# Awesome VTubing Stack

A curated list of software, hardware, and assets for virtual avatar production and live-streaming.

## Software Stack
* [VTube Studio](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1325800/VTube_Studio/) — The industry standard for animating 2D Live2D models on Windows and macOS.
* [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) — Free and open-source software for video recording and live streaming.
* [nizima LIVE](https://docs.nizimalive.com/) — Live2D's official tracking and broadcasting alternative.

## Tracking & Input Hardware
* **iPhone with Face ID** (iPhone X or newer) — Recommended input device using iOS ARKit for smooth, precise eye-tracking and facial animations.
* **USB or XLR Microphone** — High-quality audio capturing (invest here before upgrading your avatar model!).

## Avatar Creation & Rigging
* [VRoid Studio](https://vroid.com/en/studio) — Free 3D character maker exporting to VRM format.
* [Live2D Cubism](https://www.live2d.com/en/) — Professional toolset for creating layered, deformable 2D character models.


Default Mac Software Stack: VTube Studio (with watermark removal) and OBS Studio
.
Alternative Software: nizima LIVE (Live2D's official tracker), Animaze (Windows-only), VSeeFace (3D VRM), Warudo (3D VRM), and newer AI-assisted avatar tools
.
Tracking & Hardware Assets: iPhone (X or newer with Face ID/ARKit for eye and mouth tracking), tripod mounts, backup webcams, and USB/XLR microphones
.
Avatar Design Ecosystem: Live2D rigs (for 2D talking-head setups), and VRoid Studio, Blender, or Unity (for 3D VRM formats)
.
